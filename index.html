6 September 2026

Class Update

In today's class, the questions and answers for next week's oral quiz were discussed with the students.

Oral Quiz – Next Week

Please revise the following topics:

  • Usul al-Din
  • Furu al-Din
  • The Twelve Imams
  • Imam al-Mahdi (AS)
  • Daily Wajib Salah
  • Recitation in Salah

Quiz Questions

1. Usul al-Din

  • What does Usul al-Din mean?
  • How many Usul al-Din are there?
  • Name the five Usul al-Din.
  • Briefly explain each one.

2. Furu al-Din

  • What does Furu al-Din mean?
  • How many Furu al-Din are there?
  • Name the ten Furu al-Din.
  • Briefly explain each one.

3. Imamah and Imam al-Mahdi (AS)

  • How many Imams are there?
  • Name the twelve Imams in sequence.
  • Who is the father of Imam al-Mahdi (AS)?
  • What does it mean that Imam al-Mahdi (AS) is in Ghaybah?
  • What will Imam al-Mahdi (AS) do when he reappears?
  • Name two things believers should do while awaiting his reappearance.

4. Daily Wajib Salah

  • How many daily wajib prayers are there? Name them.
  • How many rak'ahs are in each prayer?
  • When is each daily prayer performed?

5. Recitation in Salah

  • What begins the salah after making the intention?
  • What is recited while standing in the first two rak'ahs?
  • What do we recite in ruku?
  • What do we recite in sujud?
  • What may be recited in the 3rd and 4th rak'ahs?
  • What do we recite near the end of the salah?

Please help and encourage students to revise these questions before the quiz.
